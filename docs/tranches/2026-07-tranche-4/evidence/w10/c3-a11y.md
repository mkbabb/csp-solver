## GATE 1 — DIFFICULTY CONTRAST (WCAG 2.x, browser-resolved hexes, both themes)

Method: for each surface, read `getComputedStyle(el).color` (Chromium resolves `color-mix`/alpha to a concrete value), composite over the **effective backdrop** (walk ancestors, composite their `background-color`s, theme-paper fallback), then WCAG relative-luminance ratio. Backdrop resolved per element (`--color-card #fdfdfc` for the panel headings; the tally sits on the board margin `#fbfaf9`).

### The RED (LIGHT theme — the crayons collapse into wax on cream paper)

| Surface | fg (resolved) | bg | ratio | 4.5:1 | large-3:1 | owning token |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Difficulty heading — EASY** | `#2dc653` crayon-green | `#fdfdfc` | **2.22:1** | FAIL | FAIL | `--color-crayon-green` |
| **Difficulty heading — MEDIUM** | `#f4a236` crayon-orange | `#fdfdfc` | **2.05:1** | FAIL | FAIL | `--color-crayon-orange` |
| **Difficulty heading — HARD** | `#e8315b` crayon-rose | `#fdfdfc` | **4.11:1** | FAIL | pass | `--color-crayon-rose` |
| **tally `.dt-label`** ("difficulty") | `#777776` (graphite @62%) | `#fbfaf9` | **4.32:1** | FAIL | n/a (14px) | `.dt-label` color-mix 62% |
| **`.heading-value`** (mobile closed-tab value) | `#7c7c7c` (graphite @60%) | `#fdfdfc` | **4.10:1** | FAIL | n/a (12px) | `.heading-value` color-mix 60% |

The Difficulty heading is bold ≥18.66px (25.9px/800 desktop, 20.4px/800 mobile) → WCAG large-text 3:1 sub-floor applies; **green + orange fail even the 3:1 large floor** (unambiguous), rose clears 3:1 but fails the gate's 4.5:1. Both games render the same heading tint (measured: green on futoshiki-default EASY 2.22, rose on sudoku-default HARD 4.11; orange 2.05 driven live by selecting MEDIUM).

### The PASS rows (context — not the gate, but banked)
- New-game / Size headings + unselected options: muted-foreground `#737373` → **4.66:1** (light). PASS.
- **Selected** difficulty *option* (OptionSelector button): renders `#0a0a0a` **19.45:1** — NOT the crayon. Reason: `opt.colorClass="crayon-rose"` is passed to `OptionSelector.vue`, but `.crayon-*` is defined **only in `ControlPanel.vue`'s scoped `<style>` (`:792-800`)**, so the class is inert inside `OptionSelector`'s subtree → falls back to inherited foreground. The wax tint lands **only on the heading** (ControlPanel's own scope). So the option labels are fine; the **heading is the sole option-zone contrast fail**.
- **DARK theme: every difficulty surface PASSES** — the crayons glow at night (green 10.09:1, rose 6.30:1, muted 7.68:1, dt-label 5.26:1, heading-value 4.96:1). **The lift is LIGHT-theme-only.**
- `.dt-name` (the technique name, graphite @72%, revealed on hover/focus) composites to ≈`#626262` ≈5.7:1 — **passes** when shown; collapsed at rest. Not a fail.

### Fix lever (ink-tier doctrine, T3-W9 §1 — a passing ink hex, not a wax tone)
1. **Difficulty heading** — the design intent is difficulty-coded color (green/orange/rose). Keep the semantic, swap the LIGHT-theme heading ink from raw wax to a **hue-locked darkened ink tier** (the exact move `--color-red-ink #d02a52` = 4.87:1 already models for rose). Mint `--color-green-ink` / `--color-orange-ink` analogues (or a single heading token per tier) in `index.css :root`, point the heading's `.crayon-*` at the ink tier under light, and let dark keep the wax (it passes). Owning sites: `ControlPanel.vue:792-800` (both games), tokens `index.css:149-151`.
2. **`.dt-label`** — raise the color-mix from `62%` graphite toward the transparent-free end (≈78%+ clears 4.5:1). Site: `DifficultyTally.vue:244-253`.
3. **`.heading-value`** — same lever, `60%`→higher. Site: `ControlPanel.vue:967-977`.

## GATE 2 — FOCUS / FORCED-COLORS

Born-RED confirmed: `grep -rn "forced-colors\|forced-color-adjust\|@media (forced" web/frontend/src` → **one hit, a comment** (`SudokuCell.vue:698`). No `@media (forced-colors: active)` block, no `forced-color-adjust` declaration anywhere. FutoshikiCell has no forced-colors mention at all.

### Normal mode — every interactive target DOES paint a `:focus-visible` indicator (keyboard tab-walk)

| Target | :focus-visible indicator (normal) |
|---|---|
| `.sun-moon-toggle`, `.logo-trigger` | `outline: 2px solid` (explicit) — visible |
| `.difficulty-tally` | `outline: 2px solid #3a7bc4` (explicit, `DifficultyTally.vue:322`) — visible |
| `.drawer-tab` | `outline: 2px dashed currentColor` (explicit, `DrawerTab.vue:116`) — visible |
| `.ctrl-btn` (OptionSelector / Pencil-mode / Assist chips), `.icon-btn`, links | `outline-style: auto` (UA-drawn ring) — visible |
| **board cell `<input>`** | `outline-style: none` → input paints **nothing**; the cue is the SVG `.cell-ghost-path` (crayon-blue `#3a7bc4`, stroke 7px, opacity 0.9) — visible (see `focus-cell-normal-light.png`, full soft-blue ring) |

So "no ring in normal mode" is **not** the defect. The defect is the forced-colors regression on the **board cells**.

### Forced-colors active (`forcedColors: 'active'`, Chromium HC emulation) — what survives

- **Chrome controls survive.** Explicit `solid`/`dashed` and UA `auto` outlines all repaint with the forced system color (`rgba(5,0,73,0.8)` = emulated CanvasText): difficulty-tally 2px solid ✓, drawer-tab 2px dashed ✓, ctrl-btn/icon-btn 2px `auto` ✓. Chrome is fine.
- **Board CELLS = the RED.** The input's outline is `outline-style: none` → **a `none` outline cannot be restored by forced-colors** (it paints nothing, in any HC theme). The `SudokuCell.vue:696-702` comment asserts `outline-none` is *"a transparent 2px outline"* that HC repaints — **FALSE under Tailwind v4.3.2**: the built CSS is `.outline-none{--tw-outline-style:none;outline-style:none}` (v4 changed the utility from v3's `2px solid transparent`). The comment encodes stale v3 behavior; the promised fallback does not exist.
  - The only remaining cue, the SVG `.cell-ghost-path`, is **largely stripped by forced-color-adjust** — captures show only a **small blue corner fragment** survives on the focused cell (`focus-cell-forcedcolors-light.png`, `-dark.png`), NOT the full ring of normal mode. Cells have no robust focus indicator in HC.

Evidence: `focus-cell-normal-light.png` (full ring) vs `focus-cell-forcedcolors-{light,dark}.png` (corner fragment only); `forcedcolors-fullpage-{light,dark}.png` (chrome-control rings survive). Computed-style dump: `c3-focus-raw.json`.

### Fix lever (T3-W9 §3.2 — the ring owns crayon-blue; do NOT tint it gold)
Give the cell input a **non-SVG focus outline that forced-colors keeps**. Two equivalent forms:
- add `@media (forced-colors: active){ .cell-native-input:focus-visible{ outline: 2px solid Highlight; outline-offset: -2px } }` (system color, both games' cells), OR
- replace the Tailwind `outline-none` on the input with a real `outline: 2px solid transparent` (restores v3's HC-repaint behavior) — but the explicit `@media` form is the honest, self-documenting fix.

Retire/correct the stale premise in `SudokuCell.vue:696-702` and add the twin to `FutoshikiCell.vue` (input `:307`, `:focus-within` `:677-680`). Owning sites: `SudokuCell.vue:315` / `FutoshikiCell.vue:307` (the `outline-none` input class).

### Recipe
```
node c3-focus.mjs   # normal tab-walk + forcedColors:'active' cell/chrome probes + capture PNGs
```

## GATE 3 — 320px REFLOW (WCAG 1.4.10)

Method: desktop-context viewport width 320 (innerWidth locked to 320, no mobile zoom-out), `document.scrollingElement.scrollWidth` vs `clientWidth`, then enumerate elements whose right edge exceeds `innerWidth`.

| Game @ 320 | innerW | scrollW | overflow | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **sudoku** | 320 | 320 | **0px** | **PASS** — board scales to fit |
| **futoshiki** | 320 | 326 | **+6px** | **FAIL** — horizontal overflow |

**Overflow element chain (futoshiki):**
`main.main-content` (320px, `padding: 0 4px` → 312px content) › **`div.board-group` (331.9px, `min-width: auto`, `display:flex`, `overflow:visible` — min-content-driven, `App.vue:270`)** › `div.app-layout` / `h1.masthead` / `div.logo-menu` / `button.logo-trigger` all stretched to 331.9px.

Root driver: the **FutoshikiBoard's intrinsic min-content width ≈332px** (5×5 cells + inequality-sign gutters + `board-margin`) exceeds the 320 viewport. `.mobile-board-width` (`min(42rem, 100vw − 1.5rem)` = 296px at 320, `scene.css:120`) is **not** the driver. `.board-group` has no width cap, so it grows to min-content and the whole column overflows. Visually: board-group centers in 320 → shifts left −5.9px, clipping the "**f**utoshiki" wordmark (`reflow-320-futoshiki.png`, red guide at x=320). Sudoku's board scales below 320, so it's clean (`reflow-320-sudoku.png`).

### Fix lever
Make the futoshiki board fit ≤320: cap `.board-group` (or the board) to `100%`/`max-width` and let the board scale — reduce the board's intrinsic min at narrow widths (viewport-relative cell + inequality-gutter + margin sizing) so `board-group ≤ innerWidth − main-content padding` (≈312px at 320). Sudoku is the working reference.


## GATE 4 — TARGET SIZE (WCAG 2.5.5 AAA 44px / 2.5.8 AA 24px)

Tap target = the cell's native `<input>` (`absolute inset-0 h-full w-full` → exactly the cell box). `getBoundingClientRect()` on the input, min over all visible cells. deviceScaleFactor 2, `hasTouch`.

| Board | @ 390 | @ 320 | vs 44 (AAA) | vs 24 (AA) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **sudoku 9×9** | **40.22px** | **32.44px** | FAIL both | **PASS both** |
| **sudoku 16×16** | ~22.63px | **~18.25px** | FAIL both | @390 pass · **@320 FAIL (18.25 < 24)** |
| **futoshiki 5×5** | **72.39px** | **58.39px** | **PASS both** | **PASS both** |

Notes:
- **futoshiki 5×5 clears 44px** comfortably (few large cells) — no action.
- **sudoku 9×9** misses 44 at both widths but **clears the AA 24px floor**. 44px is arithmetically impossible here: 9×44 = 396 > 320 (a 9-cell row cannot be 44px each in a 320 column). `index.css:618` already exempts board cells from the coarse 44px floor (`@media (pointer: coarse)`, `:621-628`) on exactly this ground (D12).
- **sudoku 16×16 @ 320 = 18.25px breaches even the AA 24px minimum** (16×24 = 384 > 320 → 24px also unreachable without a scrolling board). This is the one genuine sub-AA breach.
- Measurement caveat: at 16×16, **12 of 256 cells reported 0×0** (a transient grid-collapse artifact under the dense flex layout; `parentDisplay:grid`, `display:flex`) — excluded from the min; the representative cell is the bulk figure (22.63/18.25). Does not change any verdict. See `c3-rt2-raw.json`.

### Fix lever (spec: grow HIT AREA, not glyph)
The input already fills the cell edge-to-edge in a packed grid, so hit-area cannot extend without overlapping neighbors — the only levers are board-geometry:
- reclaim board horizontal padding/margin to enlarge cells a few px (helps 9×9 toward, never to, 44);
- for the 16×16@320 <24 AA breach: floor the board so cells never drop below 24px (accept a horizontally-scrolling board at that density) **or** gate 16×16 out of the ≤320 regime.
Honest read: 5×5 passes 44; **9×9 clears AA 24 but 44 is geometrically capped**; **16×16@320 is the real AA breach**. Whatever the implementation lane commits, the achievable floor at these widths is AA 24px, not AAA 44px, for 9×9/16×16.
