--- 2 · WHICH SURFACE THE TOOLCHAIN ACTUALLY CONSUMES (measured) ------------

The row said to pick the surface the toolchain consumes and VERIFY BY BUILD OUTPUT.
So it was verified, not assumed: two `vite build` runs into separate outDirs, same
source, differing only by the presence of the browserslist key.

  $ npx vite build --outDir dist-probe-a --emptyOutDir     # no browserslist
  $ <add browserslist to package.json>
  $ npx vite build --outDir dist-probe-b --emptyOutDir     # with browserslist
  $ (cd dist-probe-X && find . -type f | sort | xargs shasum -a 256 | shasum -a 256)

    A (no browserslist)  b17917ad46240b21f3787144faa107f68c180353c3108dd5663495cd9fcf7db0
    B (browserslist)     b17917ad46240b21f3787144faa107f68c180353c3108dd5663495cd9fcf7db0
    44 files, file-by-file diff empty. EQUAL.

browserslist is INERT to this toolchain. Vite reads `build.target`; Tailwind v4
compiles CSS against targets hard-coded in @tailwindcss/node. Neither consults it.
That is not a reason to omit the declaration — gates.json W1 names browserslist as
the declared surface, and it is where a human looks — it is the reason checks 2 and
3 exist: they bind the declaration to the two floors the toolchain DOES consume, in
the only safe direction (compiled floors may sit under the promise, never over it).
A declaration no gate reads would be decoration; this one reds, seven ways.
The probe dirs were removed; `dist*/` is eslint-ignored, so neither entered any lint
or golden set while it existed.

OPEN, DELIBERATELY NOT TAKEN IN THIS ROW: `build.target` could be driven off the
declaration (es2020 -> es2022 is legal at this floor and would shrink the bundle).
That changes every emitted byte and would collide with the byte-band and golden
lanes other W1 rows own. Recorded for a later wave, not smuggled into this diff.
