## A. FINDINGS, ranked

### H1 — `role="grid"` owns 81 `gridcell`s with no `row` layer (all five games)

`src/games/shared/GameBoard.vue:729-743` — the grid container.
`src/games/sudoku/SudokuBoard/SudokuBoard.vue:185-190` — the `#cells` slot renders a flat `v-for="pos in totalCells"`; the four sibling boards do the same.

Live AX tree, playing view:

```
PLAYING {"grid":1, "gridcell":81, "textbox":81, ...}   ← no "row" role anywhere
GRIDCELL0 children: [{"role":"textbox","name":"Row 1, column 1, given clue 4"}, …]
```

`grep -rn 'role="row"' src` returns zero hits.

**User impact.** A screen-reader user who enters the board in table/browse mode gets nothing: `gridcell` requires an owning `row`, so NVDA and JAWS cannot report "row 3 column 7", cannot do Ctrl+Alt+arrow table navigation, and may drop the grid semantics entirely. The per-cell `aria-label` carries the coordinates as prose, which is why the board is *usable* — but the structure the ARIA claims does not exist, and the ARIA-required-children rule fails on every board in the estate.

### H2 — the destructive-work guard is silent to assistive tech, and the next Enter destroys the board

`src/pencil/chrome/GameGallery/GameGallery.vue:596-603` — `role="alertdialog"`, `aria-label` only.
`src/pencil/chrome/GameGallery/GameGallery.vue:431-443` — while armed, `Enter`/`Space` resolves `guardLeave()`.

Live probe (dirty sudoku board → `g` → ArrowRight → Enter):

```
GUARD {"present":true,"label":"Leave this puzzle?","ariaModal":null,"describedby":null,
       "activeAfterArm":"listbox","activeInsideDialog":false,
       "liveRegionText":"futoshiki, 2 of 5. 5×5 easy, new game"}
```

Focus never enters the dialog, there is no `aria-modal`, no `aria-describedby` onto `.guard-note-text`, and the polite `.gallery-live` region (`:635`) says nothing about the ribbon. `alertdialog` carries no implicit live-region semantics — an alertdialog that never receives focus is announced by nothing.

**User impact.** A blind player with marks on the board presses Enter to switch games; the ribbon appears, nothing is spoken, the app appears frozen. They press Enter again — and that second Enter is the confirm. Their work is discarded without the warning ever having been said aloud. The guard protects sighted users only.

### H3 — the game picker exposes 1 of its 5 options to assistive tech

`src/pencil/chrome/GameGallery/GameCard.vue:229-236` — `role="option"` + `:inert="!isActive || undefined"`.

Live AX tree with the gallery open:

```
GALLERY {"listbox":1, "option":1, ...}
GALLERY OPTIONS ["sudoku, 1 of 5"]
```

DOM has all five `role="option"` nodes with resolving `aria-activedescendant` (`gallery-card-0`, no dangling IDREFs) — but `inert` removes the four flanks from the accessibility tree, so Chrome publishes a one-item listbox.

**User impact.** A screen-reader user opening the picker is told they are in a listbox with a single choice. They cannot review the deck, cannot hear futoshiki/thermo/killer/kenken exist, and cannot use the listbox reading commands their AT gives them. Arrow keys do still work (the handler is on the viewport at `:537` and the polite region re-reads on each snap), so the deck is *operable* — it is not *browsable*, and nothing tells them to keep pressing arrows.

### M4 — the `K` peek shortcut swallows Ctrl+K / Cmd+K and is scoped to nothing

`src/games/shared/useAnswerKeyPeek.ts:55-70` — a window-level `keydown` with no `metaKey`/`ctrlKey`/`altKey` guard, calling `e.preventDefault()` at `:66`.

Live probe, focus in a board cell, `Control+k` pressed:

```
CTRL_K {"laminate":true,"peekAnnounce":"peeking at the answer key"}
```

Contrast with the two handlers that got this right: `src/App.vue:438-447` (`g`, returns on any modifier, exempts editable targets) and `src/pencil/chrome/GameGallery/GameGallery.vue:482` (`d`, same guard).

**User impact.** Ctrl+K (Chrome's search-from-address-bar) and Cmd+K are eaten by the page and instead flash the answer key over the player's board. Separately, `k`, `g`, `h`, `p` and `d` are single-character shortcuts with no off switch and no remap; `k` is not even scoped to a focused component (the `.board-cells` exemption was deliberately removed at `:61-65`), which is a plain WCAG 2.1.4 Character Key Shortcuts (Level A) failure for anyone driving the page by voice.

### M5 — every filled cell announces its digit twice

`src/pencil/glyph/HandwrittenGlyph.vue:321-327` — `<svg … :aria-label="value">` with no `role`, no `aria-hidden`.

Live AX tree, one gridcell:

```
GRIDCELL0 children: [{"role":"textbox","name":"Row 1, column 1, given clue 4"},
                     {"role":"image","name":"4"},
                     {"role":"generic","name":""}]
```

62 such `image` nodes on a dealt 9×9 (`labelledSvgNoRoleByClass {"glyph-svg":62}`). The value is already in the cell's accessible name, derived at `src/games/shared/useGameCell.ts:114-135`.

**User impact.** Arrowing across a filled row, the reader hears "Row 1 column 1, given clue 4 … graphic, 4" for every cell — the digit twice, once labelled and once bare. On a 16×16 board that is up to 256 redundant utterances per pass.

### M6 — 93 unnamed graphics sit in the accessibility tree, 81 of them inside the gridcells

`src/games/sudoku/SudokuBoard/SudokuCell/SudokuCell.vue:282-289` and its Futoshiki twin `FutoshikiCell.vue` — the `.cell-ghost` focus/hover/invalid ring SVG carries no `aria-hidden` and no ancestor that does.

Live counts: `image` role = 155 in the playing view, of which 62 are the named glyphs above. Bare-SVG census by class:

```
bareSvgByClass {"absolute":81, "icon-btn":6, "outline-svg":5, "toggle-icon":2,
                "hand-drawn-grid":1, "boil-divider-wrap":1, "sparkle-icon":1, "?":1}
```

The estate is otherwise scrupulous about this (`aria-hidden="true"` on the cage layers, caret layer, thermo tube, celebration star/heart, vignette, SvgFilters) — these are the leaks.

**User impact.** Browsing the board or the control card emits an unlabelled "graphic" between meaningful items — 81 of them on the grid alone — which is the difference between a board a screen-reader user can read and one they abandon.

### M7 — the drawer moves focus like a dialog while being neither dialog nor named region

`src/games/shared/GameScene.vue:103-116` — `#controls-drawer` is a bare `<div>`: no `role`, no `aria-label`.
`src/games/shared/useControlsDrawer.ts:278-284` — `focusPanel()` puts focus on the panel's first control at open-settle.
`src/games/shared/DrawerTab.vue:34-35` — `aria-expanded` + `aria-controls="controls-drawer"`.

Live probe:

```
DRAWER_CLOSED {"expanded":"false","railInert":true,"railRole":null,"railLabel":null,"active":"drawer-tab"}
DRAWER_OPEN   {"expanded":"true","active":"BUTTON"}
```

The mechanics are right — `aria-expanded` is truthful at click, `inert` at closed-idle kills the invisible tab stops, focus returns to the tab on close, Esc closes from within.

**User impact.** The one gap is the arrival: a user who opens the pencil case is teleported to "Deal a new board" with no announcement of what they entered. `aria-controls` names a region that has no accessible name to announce, so nothing bridges the jump. Either name the rail (`role="region"` + `aria-label="controls"`, or `aria-labelledby` the tab) or stop moving focus.

### M8 — the coarse tap-target floor is width-blind for the section tabs

`src/games/shared/GameControlPanel.vue:425` — `.mobile-heading-btn`, CSS at `:1260-1269` with `padding: 0`.
`src/assets/index.css:773-779` grants `min-height: 2.75rem` to `.mobile-heading-btn`; the `min-width: 2.75rem` added at `:786-788` covers `.ctrl-btn` **only**.

Measured live at 390×844, `pointer: coarse`, after animations settle — the sole sub-44 control on the page:

```
MOBILE390 {"under44":[{"label":"Size","w":41.2,"h":44}], "cellPx":40.2, "coarse":true}
```

**User impact.** The "Size" tab is 41.2px wide on a phone — the same defect class the T4-P1 note at `index.css:781-788` says it closed, reappearing on the one surface that rule did not name. A user with a tremor mis-taps into the Difficulty tab. (Board cells at 40.2px are the documented arithmetic exemption, `GameBoard.vue:220-232` — not a finding.)

### M9 — `<h2>` nested inside `<button>`

`src/games/shared/GameControlPanel.vue:425-438` (mobile twin) and `:676-684` (rail twin) — a `<button>` whose child is an `<h2 class="section-heading" :aria-label>`.

`<button>`'s content model is phrasing content; `<h2>` is flow content. The AX tree carries `heading: 3` in the playing view, so the heading role survives inside the control.

**User impact.** Heading navigation (H key in a screen reader) lands the user *inside* an interactive control, where the next keystroke is ambiguous. Parser behaviour for invalid nesting is not guaranteed across engines, so the accessible name of the tab is engine-dependent.

### L10 — the keyboard legend misstates redo, and is hidden exactly where a keyboard user needs it

`src/pencil/chrome/KeyboardLegend.vue:33-36` prints `⇧ Z` → redo. Source requires the modifier first: `src/games/shared/GameBoard.vue:461-470` — the `z` case only fires under `e.ctrlKey || e.metaKey`, then branches on `e.shiftKey`. **Shift+Z alone does nothing.**
`KeyboardLegend.vue:47` gates the whole legend behind `(hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)`; it is mounted only in the rail twin (`GameControlPanel.vue:895`), which is `inert` whenever the drawer is closed (`GameScene.vue:108`).
No `aria-keyshortcuts` exists for `h`, `p`, `k` or Ctrl+Z anywhere; `d` is the only advertised one (`GameGallery.vue:535`, `StagingBand.vue:146`).

**User impact.** A keyboard-only player is told a redo chord that does not work, and a player who tucked the drawer away loses the crib entirely. Arrow / Home / End / Ctrl+Home board navigation is documented in no user-facing surface at all.

### L11 — five orphan `role="tooltip"` nodes float in the accessibility tree

`src/pencil/sheet/SheetWashiLabel.vue:72` — `:role="anchor === 'tag' || persistent ? undefined : 'tooltip'"`. The five non-tag, non-persistent tapes are `GameControlPanel.vue:710, 815, 826, 842, 853` (Deal / Clear / fill forced / Solve / Share).
`grep -rn 'aria-describedby' src` → **zero hits**. Nothing references them.
Live AX tree: `"tooltip": 5`.

**User impact.** A reader walking the control card hits five unattached "tooltip" nodes whose text duplicates the buttons' own `aria-label`s. A tooltip that no control points at is loose furniture in the tree.

### L12 — a dead tab stop on the difficulty tally

`src/games/shared/DifficultyTally.vue:196-202` — `role="img"` + `tabindex="0"`. The hover/focus reveal this tab stop existed to trigger was retired at pass 4; the component's own comment at `:248-263` records the retirement. The `tabindex` stayed.

Live tab order at 1440 confirms it: `{"t":"div","role":"img","label":"difficulty — singles only (1 of 5)","ti":"0"}`, between "Deal a new board" and "Normal".

**User impact.** One Tab press in the middle of the control cluster lands on something that does nothing and offers nothing to activate. Its name is already announced; the stop buys the user nothing and costs them a keystroke every pass.

### L13 — two controls live outside every landmark, and there is no skip link

`src/App.vue:463-468` — `AttributionCard` and the `.corner-right` `DarkModeToggle` are siblings *before* `<main>` (`:470`). Live landmarks: `["main"]`. Live `focusableOutsideMain`: `["Show attribution card", "Switch to dark mode"]`. No `<header>`, no `<nav>`, no `<footer>`, no skip link.

**User impact.** Landmark navigation reaches only the main region, so the theme toggle and the attribution card are findable by linear Tab alone. Low harm here — the page has 25 tab stops total and the two orphans are the first two — but it is why `document.body` region-jumping does not work.

### L14 — `role="separator"` carries a pointer-only gesture

`src/games/shared/GameControlPanel.vue:496-505` (and the twin at `:727-740`) — a `<div role="separator">` with pointerdown/move/up/leave/cancel handlers, no `tabindex`, no keydown, and a 100-character `aria-label` advertising "press and hold, or press K, to peek at the answer key".

**User impact.** The separator announces an interaction it cannot receive. The `K` alternative is real (`useAnswerKeyPeek.ts:60-69`), which is what keeps this off the 2.1.1 list — but the affordance and the mechanism are on different elements, and the label is a paragraph read aloud in full every time the reader crosses the divider.

### L15 — focus indication on the main control cluster is UA default

`src/games/shared/GameControlPanel.vue` — no `:focus-visible` rule in 1,295 lines (the only `outline` mention is a comment at `:1148`). `src/pencil/chrome/OptionSelector/OptionSelector.vue` `.ctrl-btn` — none either. The estate does carve explicit rings elsewhere: `DrawerTab.vue:116`, `DarkModeToggle.vue:712`, `GameGallery.vue:676` + `:813`, `StagingBand.vue:277`, `HandwrittenLogo.vue:348`, `DifficultyTally.vue:330`, `gameCell.css:202`.

No global `outline: none` reset exists (the two hits, `gameCell.css:259` and `DarkModeToggle.vue:704/709`, are scoped and each substitutes a drawn ring), so nothing is invisible.

**User impact.** ~20 of the page's 25 tab stops — every icon button, every option chip — show the browser's default ring over hand-drawn paper rather than the crayon-blue sketched ring the rest of the estate uses. Not a failure; an inconsistency that reads as "focus left the app's world".

## C. LIMITS OF THIS PASS

- No `axe-core` run: the package is absent from `node_modules` and adding it would have written to the lockfile, which this pass is barred from. The AX-tree dump above covers the rules that matter here (`aria-required-children`, `aria-required-parent`, `aria-hidden`/name duplication, target size, landmark and heading structure) with harder evidence than a rule id.
- Chromium only. Safari/VoiceOver and Firefox map bare `<svg>`, `inert` and `role="alertdialog"` differently; H3 in particular could be better or worse under VoiceOver and is **UNKNOWN** there.
- No live screen-reader transcript. Every announcement claim above is derived from the AX tree and the DOM, not from NVDA/VoiceOver speech.
- Killer, KenKen and Thermo boards were read statically only; they share `GameBoard.vue` and `PosterBoard.vue`, so H1, M5 and M6 are structural for them, but their bespoke clue layers (`KillerCage.vue:104`, `KenKenCage.vue:109`, `ThermoTube.vue:68` — all `aria-hidden="true"`) were not probed live.
- Contrast ratios are computed for pairs derivable from the custom properties. Composited-alpha surfaces (the laminate's milk gradient, the peer wash, the because wash) are outside that model and were **not** evaluated — the same blind spot `index.css:96-100` books for `opacity`.
