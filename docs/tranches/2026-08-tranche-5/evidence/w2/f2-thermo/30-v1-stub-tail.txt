## WHY IT SURVIVES F2 — a fence, recorded, not a miss

Killing the tail means giving thermo a REAL `?board=`. That codec is `games/shared/persistence.ts`
— one urlCodec for all five, parameterized by the clue seam's encode/decode, which `thermoSpec`
now carries. It sits under the surface this lane is fenced from, and it cannot be written
per-game without minting the sixth variant the charter forbids. It is also wave-open 2.4d, the
single disclosed behaviour break and an OWNER RATIFICATION row — not a lane's call in either
direction.

The three stubs are load-bearing today: `GameStateDomain` requires `syncToUrl`, `dropBoardParam`
and `writeShareUrl`, all non-optional (`useGameState.ts:146-159`). Deleting the bodies without
the shared codec distils nothing; it breaks the domain slot.
