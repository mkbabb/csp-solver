### 2 — The crisp storybook toggle: the Bloom on an in-SVG warp (finding 5)

> "The darkmode toggle animation renders in a low-res variant on animation — this is totally wrong. Needs a great deal of refinement, to warp in and out like a storybook-popup (shrinking and growing, etc). Design and divine this from first principles, look to how our animation was previously."

**The mechanism, confirmed by injection (b2, crit-perf re-inspected the A/B pair):** `.corner-right` carries `will-change: transform` (`App.vue:212-224`, the D1/P2 fence) and never itself moves — the whirl transforms live on the child SVG icons (`DarkModeToggle.vue:384-424`). The promoted layer's subtree — filtered SVG, `feDisplacementMap` baked in — rasters ONCE at scale 1.0; any frame where the icon exceeds 1.0 bitmap-stretches that cached texture. Onset is strictly scale > 1.0 (edge-crop threshold proven); the spring overshoot peaks at **1.088** (crit-perf re-derived; the eased max of `cubic-bezier(0.34,1.56,0.64,1)` is 1.0978 at t=0.582), the plush-land hits 1.05, hover 1.08 — exactly the crest/land frames the eye lands on. Toggling the one property flips blurry↔crisp with everything else held (`static-promoted-1_6.png` vs `static-unpromoted-1_6.png`). The worst case (raster at 0.1, scale up 10×) does NOT occur — the parked icon rests `visibility: hidden`, so no small cache is ever minted.

**THE RULING (crit-design kill #1 — the blocking cross-lane decision, resolved here):** b3's in-SVG warp and b2's option B (over-raster the cached texture, compositor-scale it) are mutually exclusive. **This wave ships the WARP.** The mechanics: a `<g class="warp">` wraps each icon's full content INSIDE the filtered `<svg>` (`transform-box: view-box; transform-origin: 100px 100px`); the big scale ride animates the `<g>`, which changes the filter's INPUT — every frame is a fresh vector render at device resolution, crisp at scale 0.06 and 1.088 alike, on both toggle rungs. Rationale, on the true numbers (crit-design kill #2 folded): the warp costs **~60 filter rasters/s for ~1.0s on a ~250px region — a ~7.5× transient over the 8/s rest rate**, user-initiated, once, gesture-scoped — precisely the class §1-P4 rules "correctly ephemeral"; b2-B freezes the boil mid-gesture and scales the wobble crinkle WITH the bitmap — wrong exactly at the storybook moment, where the crinkle should read as folding paper; and the warp composes with §1-P3 (rest = frozen stack, gesture = live instance, settle = stack again). **b2-B is the RECORDED FALLBACK**: if the gate's frame-time trace shows dropped frames attributable to the warp raster, over-raster the icon's intrinsic box at 1.15× (sized to the Bloom's 1.088 crest, not the old 1.10) with a compensating baseline scale, and keep the promotion.

**The crispness contract (b3 §2, binding on the implementation):** (1) any scale excursion beyond ±8% of identity rides the in-viewBox warp group; (2) CSS-layer scaling is permitted only within ±8% of 1 (button squash 0.94–1.01, plush flex 0.95–1.05 — sub-perceptual raster stretch at 208px); (3) the cost is gesture-scoped by construction; (4) feTurbulence displacement is constant in user units, so at warp scale 0.1 the wobble reads ~10× proportionally larger — **prototype call**: if legibility breaks below scale ~0.3, freeze the filter seed for the first/last 2 beats of the warp. The T3-W10 keep (filter bound on both icons, parked icon `visibility: hidden`) is untouched.

The shipped pure-`.is-active` transition architecture keeps EXACTLY (re-clicks retarget for free — the property W12 fought to restore, `DarkModeToggle.vue:299-306`); only the shapes swap. Rest pose (inactive `.warp`): `scale(0.06) rotate(12deg) translateY(6px)` — a wrung scrap pressed into the page. Active pose: identity.

### 3 — The drawer as one solid (finding 2)

> "The drawer animation is not right AT ALL and needs proper smoothing and perfecting."

**The forensics (b4, mechanism crit-confirmed at every anchor):** the glide engine's math is sound, the pipeline drops zero frames — the wrong is choreography plus one outright defect:

- **F1 — the phantom start (the killer).** The controls rail starts every glide from the WRONG layout's pose — a one-frame **~249px vertical teleport** (painted top 11.4px, up in the masthead zone), then a 480ms diagonal slosh into the tuck. Mechanism: the measure flip (`useControlsDrawer.ts:180-190`) is paint-invisible but not *recalc*-invisible — the rail's rest pose is expressed AS a transitionable `transform: translateY(-50%)` (`scene.css:50-56`), the A→B recalc pair lands while `drawer-gesturing` has armed `transition: transform` (`scene.css:67-71`), an unintended transition starts between the two layouts' poses, and the mover's inline transform retargets it from ≈the wrong end. Only the rail expresses its rest pose as a transform — only the rail sloshes.
- **F2 — two easing families**: board + masthead on the spring `(0.34,1.56,0.64,1)` (`scene.css:63`, `App.vue:310`), rail on easeOutCubic `(0.33,1,0.68,1)` (`scene.css:68`) — the sheet overshoots +8.7% and doubles back while the case travels monotonically; relative motion reverses mid-gesture, two unrelated objects.

- **F4 — settle pop**: the glide scales a pre-glide raster (1.0216) and lands the true layout in one frame at settle — 480ms soft, then a sharpness snap.
- **F5 — verified sound, keep**: FLIP math exact, frame cadence clean, tab counter-scale within 0.2% of 1, PRM same-frame swap, the full a11y contract.

**THE SCOPE RULING (crit-design kill #3):** the minimal fix (rest pose onto the `translate:` property so no `transform` animation can capture it) kills F1 alone — it leaves the easing split, the onset dead zone, and the settle pop, and the verdict was "not right AT ALL," not "it teleports." **This wave commits to the full S1–S4 re-architecture; the minimal fix rides INSIDE S1 as defense-in-depth, not beside it as an alternative.**
