ROOT CAUSE

The black arm styled a CASCADE at a surface that is a BITMAP. Its injection was a
stylesheet forcing `fill`/`stroke`/`stop-color`/`opacity`/`filter` on the descendants of
every asserted surface, plus `background:#000` on the cell and the board wrapper. Three
goldens bit on that: the cell and the grid corner flood from the backgrounds (0.73 / 0.86
of all pixels) and the crest's hidden sun un-hides on top of the moon under
`opacity:1 !important` (0.62). The WORDMARK's steady state is `image.logo-pose-bmp.is-active`
— a baked pose bitmap out of rasterPose/useRasterStack — and `fill` does nothing to a
raster. All the delta could reach there was the un-hidden measuring `<text>` (opacity 0 →
1, painting near-identical glyphs in #000 over ink already baked at #1a1a1a) and the three
inactive poses forced back to opacity 1. The diff came back a RIM on the glyph edges with
every letter's interior identical: 6,766 px of 173,568 = ratio 0.0390 measured on darwin.
Darwin's LOGO_FLOOR is the 0.017 soul floor, so it red locally and W6's verification read
true; linux's LOGO_FLOOR is the 0.05 sun-crest clause floor, so the SAME delta was a green
on the only platform CI runs. The defect is the T5-W1.13 blind band (0.017 < 0.03..0.04 <
0.05) with the acceptance test itself sitting inside it — not a broken compare, and not a
vacuous local run: an arm whose bite was 2.3× the tight floor and 0.78× the coarse one.

CURE (the ARM, never the grading — visual-golden.spec.ts §GOLDEN_DELTA=black)
The paint COVERS instead of styling. `paintAssertedSurfaces()` pins an opaque,
viewport-fixed box over each asserted surface's live rect (svg.handwritten-logo,
button.sun-moon-toggle, .board-wrapper), inflated 64 CSS px so the crops that reach outside
their anchor are covered too (the crest's is centred at 72×72 on a smaller button, then
shifted −12/+16). It lands on a bitmap, a live SVG, a canvas or whatever the surface is next
rebuilt out of, because it never asks the surface to render it; fixed boxes contribute no
scrollable overflow, so no clip the tests compute afterwards moves. Paint is the theme's
opposite — #000 on light, #fff on dark — because the config's YIQ `threshold: 0.3` is wide
enough to absorb a black-on-dark cover, and a delta the compare is entitled to ignore is the
same defect in the cure's clothes. And the arm checks itself: each anchor's centre is
hit-tested and a cover that did not land throws, which carries no `toHaveScreenshot` and is
therefore graded a SETUP ERROR by the harness rather than passing as an arm-wide green.
(The cover is deliberately NOT `pointer-events:none` — `elementFromPoint` looks straight
through a pointer-transparent box, and nothing touches the page after this arm paints.)
Captured actuals are now SOLID: toggle-crest-dark-actual.png is 144×144 at 100.0% (255,255,255);
logo-light-actual.png is 768×226 at 100.0% (0,0,0).

Second control added, report-only: scripts/golden-selfdelta.mjs now prints each red's
measured `N px, ratio R`. The grading is unchanged (every golden red, every red at a
`toHaveScreenshot`); it could not see the difference between a row that bites by a hair and
one that bites by a mile, and a row that bites by a hair on one platform is a row about to
be a green on another. That is exactly how this defect shipped.

RESIDUE FOR THE CHAIR

1. LINUX IS ARGUED, NOT MEASURED. The cure is geometric — a 100%-opaque cover over the
   crop — so its magnitude cannot depend on font hinting or AA the way the incumbent rim
   delta did, and the thinnest darwin row clears the linux floor 13×. It is still an
   argument until the runner speaks: the row to read on the next push is the same step in
   the e2e job, which should now print eight `ok` rows with ratios in the 0.6–0.96 band.

2. ONE FLAKE OBSERVED, ENVIRONMENTAL. During ablation A a single row red with
   `page.evaluate: Execution context was destroyed, most likely because of a navigation` —
   a Vite HMR full reload, since two other workflows were editing src/ against the same dev
   server while these runs were going. It did not recur across four subsequent full harness
   runs and cannot occur on the runner, where nothing writes to src/ mid-run. Named here so
   a future sighting is a second occurrence rather than a first.

3. THE MAGNITUDE COLUMN IS A REPORT, NOT A GATE. It prints; it grades nothing. If the chair
   wants the blind band closed by construction rather than by eye, the ruling to write is a
   floor on the ratio each arm must clear (e.g. ≥ 4× the coarsest floor on the estate) — a
   grading change, so it belongs to a ruling, not to this fix.

4. `.sudoku-cell` is no longer an anchor: the cell crop lives inside `.board-wrapper`'s
   cover. If a future golden crops a surface outside all three anchors, DELTA_ANCHORS is
   the one place that has to learn about it — and the self-check turns that omission into
   a loud SETUP ERROR rather than a silent green.

  0.017–0.05 band, red on darwin and GREEN on the only platform CI runs (§ROOT CAUSE above).

  GRADING. A red row measured under the floor is its own failure class — BIT, BUT INSIDE
  THE BLIND BAND — and fails the harness exactly like a did-not-bite row. A red row at the
  compare that states NO ratio is a SETUP ERROR class of its own (RED AT THE COMPARE,
  STATING NO MAGNITUDE): a row that can't say how hard it bit can't prove it bit hard
  enough. Playwright ceils its printed ratio to 2dp, so the graded number is an UPPER bound
  — a row that fails this floor fails it on the compare's most generous reading.

  now. The dictum holds: the remedy for a faint row is a stronger ARM, never a smaller
