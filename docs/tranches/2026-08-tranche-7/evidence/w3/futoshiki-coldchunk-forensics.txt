── 5. THE MECHANISM, CAUGHT ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  30 unaided webkit iterations with document-level CAPTURE listeners on mousedown/mouseup/click
  and a MutationObserver on `html[class]`. Only reds print:

    iter 19 RED  preBox=[130,6,424,112] postBox=[130,6,424,112] url=?size=3&difficulty=EASY
        mouseup@523->image.logo-pose-bmp[130,6,424,112]
    iter 20–25  green
    iter 26 RED  preBox=[130,6,424,112] postBox=[130,6,424,112] url=?size=3&difficulty=EASY
        mousedown@554->text.logo-text[130,6,424,112]
    iter 27–30  green
    FAILS 2/30  [engine=webkit]

  THE SMOKING GUN, and it is unambiguous:

    iter 19 — a lone `mouseup`, no `mousedown`, NO `click`.
    iter 26 — a lone `mousedown`, no `mouseup`, NO `click`.

  WebKit synthesizes `click` only from a mousedown/mouseup PAIR. One half of the pair never
  reached the document, so no `click` was ever dispatched, so Vue's
  `@click.stop="inertHeading || emit('open')"` never ran. Everything downstream — the 200ms
  beat, `openGallery`, `?view=gallery`, `.gallery-viewport` — was correctly never reached.

  The wordmark's box is IDENTICAL before and after ([130,6,424,112] both times, and on both
  reds), so this is NOT a layout/geometry race and not the post-font re-measure.

  What differs between the two halves is the NODE UNDER THE POINTER. The surviving event in
  iter 19 landed on `image.logo-pose-bmp`; in iter 26 on `text.logo-text`. Those are the two
  ARMS of the wordmark's pose stack (HandwrittenLogo.vue):

      <template v-if="logoBaked">
        <image v-for="(url, f) in logoUrls" class="logo-pose-bmp" …/>      ← baked arm
      </template>
      <g v-for="(pid, f) in logoBaked ? [] : logoPoseIds" class="logo-pose" …>
        <text class="logo-text" …>{{ label }}</text>                        ← live-filter arm
      </g>

  When the raster bake lands, `logoBaked` flips and the ENTIRE live `<g>` stack is torn out of
  the document and replaced by `<image>` siblings. A mouse pair that straddles that swap loses
  the half whose target is destroyed, and WebKit drops the gesture silently.

  `loadApp` waits on `svg.handwritten-logo` — the earliest instant the wordmark exists in the
  DOM, well inside the bake window. That is why THIS file carries the family: rows :75, :100
  and :134 press the wordmark immediately after that wait with nothing in between, and :48
  waits only for `.sudoku-cell` to be visible, which lands in the same window. Every other
  gallery-opening spec first waits out a DEALT board (a11y's `armGuard` polls
  `.game-cell .glyph-svg > 0`; gallery-guard and permalink fill a cell first), which spends the
  bake window before pressing. The concentration is a property of the wait, not of futoshiki.

  ENGINE SCOPE — 25 chromium iterations, same rig, same path: 0 dropped presses.
  The defect is WebKit-only, which is exactly why the chromium CI job stayed green on rows the
  webkit shard red.

  ARM B IS ADOPTED. Arm A passes but is a proxy: it asserts a rendering artifact of the
  wordmark, it cannot see a LATER re-bake (`retainedPoseUrls` drops poses whenever
  `label`/`vbWidth` change), and it cures nothing if a press is dropped for any other reason.
  Arm B polls the INVARIANT the test actually needs — the deck is open — which is the
  sleep-lint's own law, and it is robust to cause.

  Idempotence, checked before adopting: `openGallery()` returns early on `view === 'gallery'`
  (useGameGallery.ts), and the wordmark renders a `<span>` rather than a `<button>` once the
  deck commits (`:inert-heading="view === 'gallery'"`, App.vue:610). A re-press therefore costs
  one beat and can never double-toggle; the inert `<span>` is also the arm that distinguishes a
  LATE open from a dropped press, and `openDeck` reads it as such.

ADDENDUM §10 — THE CLASS INVARIANT (2026-08-03, chair ruling on §8 residue item 2)

The ruling: a timing-accident shield is not a contract, and the wandering family's one-off reds
(`gallery-deal:485`, `permalink:52`, `a11y:490`) are ≥2nd occurrences. The guard is extracted
and every unguarded press in the class is converted.


── 10.1 THE HELPER ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  web/frontend/e2e/committed-press.ts — a module sibling of `wire.ts` / `ch62-admission.ts`,
  the e2e tree's existing sharing idiom.

  CONTRACT
    pressCommitted(trigger: Locator, committed: Committed, opts?) => Promise<number>
      Committed = Locator (⇒ "this is visible") | (() => Promise<boolean>)
      opts = { beatMs = 4000, budgetMs = 20000, what?: string }

    GUARANTEES the press's asserted effect is true on return. Does NOT guarantee one press,
    and callers must not depend on that. Returns the count actually sent, so a run can measure
    the drop rate instead of assuming it.

    Presses until `committed`, bounded by `budgetMs`, each attempt given `beatMs`. If the
    trigger stops resolving it is treated as INERT-ON-SUCCESS (the wordmark renders a `<span>`
    once the deck commits) and the helper waits the effect out rather than pressing a control
    that no longer exists.

    CALLER'S OBLIGATION: `committed` MUST BE FALSE BEFORE THE PRESS. See §10.3 — this is the
    defect the conversion's first cut shipped, and it is written into the module header.

    No fixed waits: every wait is a retrying Playwright wait or `expect.poll`.

  THE CRITERION FOR CONVERTING A PRESS, and it is structural rather than taste. Convert iff a
  TEAR-OUT can occur under the pointer — the shape that destroys the node mid-gesture:

      v-for="… Baked ? [] : POSES"

  Estate-wide census, re-derived 2026-08-03 (`grep -rn 'v-for="[^"]*? \[\] :' src/`):

      src/pencil/chrome/HandwrittenLogo/HandwrittenLogo.vue:392   logoBaked ? [] : logoPoseIds
      src/pencil/celestial/DarkModeToggle.vue:212                 sunBaked  ? [] : SPARKLE_POSES
      src/pencil/celestial/DarkModeToggle.vue:287                 moonBaked ? [] : STAR_POSES_D

  THREE sites, TWO components. Nothing else in the estate tears out.

  THE COUNTER-EXAMPLE IS THE PRODUCT-SIDE CURE. `HandDrawnGrid.vue` and `HandDrawnOutline.vue`
  bake exactly the same way and are NOT in the family: the live stack STAYS MOUNTED and is
  hidden (`baked-hidden`, `display:none`) while the baked `<image>` siblings paint. No node is
  ever destroyed under a pointer. That is the estate's own immune grammar, already shipped, one
  component over from the defect — and it is the shape §8.7 residue item 1 should take.

── 10.3 THE CONVERSION'S OWN DEFECT, CAUGHT BY ABLATION ────────────────────────────────────

  The first cut of `pressCommitted` opened its loop with an "already committed ⇒ nothing to do"
  fast path. `gallery-deal.spec.ts:699` ("safe verb: a wandered pair cannot OUTLIVE the
  transaction") went RED on it, and the ablation is unambiguous:

      guarded (first cut)          1 failed × 4 runs
      unguarded (HEAD shape)       1 passed × 4 runs
      variant B (locator.waitFor
        instead of waitForSelector) 1 passed × 2 runs   ⇒ the wait was never the difference

  Instrumented at the site, the cause printed itself:

      PROBE after cancel: {"bandCount":1,"bandVisible":true,"galleryCount":1,
                           "triggerCount":1,"url":"?size=3&difficulty=EASY"}
      PROBE presses: 0

  That row re-opens the deck after a cancel. Immediately after
  `expect('.game-gallery').toBeHidden()` PASSED, `.staging-band` still read **visible** — the
  deck is mid-leave, and `.game-gallery` reaches a hidden state while its descendant band still
  has a box. The fast path saw a true probe, sent ZERO presses, the deck never re-opened, and
  the row correctly observed the stale 4×4 chips it exists to forbid. The guard had turned a
  real assertion into a skipped one — the worst failure mode available to a harness cure.

  TWO FIXES, both landed:
    1. The fast path is GONE. `pressCommitted` always sends at least one press unless the
       trigger is already inert. The caller's obligation is now written into the module header
       with this row as its birth certificate.
    2. `gallery-deal`'s four sites probe `?view=gallery` — the URL truth `openGallery` writes
       and `cancel` deletes — through a file-local `pressDeckOpen`. It is false before every one
       of those presses and true only after this one lands. The `.staging-band` wait stays as
       its own line: it is the JOURNEY's precondition, not the press's committed effect, and
       conflating the two is exactly what red.

  Re-rated after the fix: **1 passed × 4 runs** on the same row, guard in place.

  The control group did its job again (lessons-from-t2-t4: born-RED + ablation caught it,
  ceremony would not have). A cure that skips the work it was guarding is not a cure.
