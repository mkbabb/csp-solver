3 · THE THIRTEEN, EACH ON ITS OWN INVARIANT
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The idiom is the wave's: poll the invariant the ASSERTION names, never a clock.
Where a whole read is one-shot, the verdict itself retries and carries its measured
figures, so a red names what it saw (`drawer.spec.ts`'s settled-read precedent).
Where the surface is continuously moving, the read is taken until two consecutive
samples AGREE and only then handed to an assertion — a settle, not a deadline.

3.1 a11y.spec.ts:156 — `armGuard()`, the 700ms before the select
    ASSERTED INVARIANT: `Enter` selects whatever the deck has COMMITTED. Cured with
    two auto-retrying attribute assertions on the commit itself — the listbox's
    `aria-activedescendant` reading `gallery-card-1` and that card's
    `aria-selected` reading true. The sleep could not red: a press sent into an
    uncommitted deck re-selects sudoku, arms no guard, and every caller of the
    helper inherited the race.

3.2 a11y.spec.ts:465 → :477 — the Ctrl+K / Cmd+K peek, TAGGED `sleep-ok`
    The one site in the thirteen where the elapsed time IS the subject, and the
    only tag minted here. The claim is an ABSENCE across a window — the laminate
    must never lay down — so there is no state to poll toward:
    `expect.poll(…).toBe(0)` greens on its first tick against a peek that simply
    has not arrived yet, which is a tautology, not a cure. This is the shape of the
    table's own `gallery.spec.ts:238` exemption ("nothing advances across several
    ~8Hz beats"). It is honest here because the row carries its own positive
    control ten lines up: bare `k` is POLLED to `laminate === 1` and Escape polled
    back to 0 in the same test, so the key pipeline is proven live and a quiet
    window is a real silence rather than a dead surface. Reason banked inline,
    ≥12 chars, cited to the mechanism (the laminate's rAF + transition).

3.3 access.spec.ts:76 — the 120ms focus-scroll window inside `occlusionCensus()`
    ASSERTED INVARIANT: the box being censused has stopped moving. `scroll-padding-*`
    and `scroll-margin-*` land on the frame the focus schedules, and a fixed 120ms
    hands a PRE-SCROLL box to a census whose whole subject is where the box sits.
    Cured in page: the rect is re-read across animation frames and only agreement
    between two consecutive ones counts as settled, with a FRAME CAP bounding the
    loop rather than the wait. Faster in the common case (two frames ≈ 33ms against
    120ms fixed, per control, times every tabbable) and it can red — a box that
    never settles is measured where it ended, not silently at frame one.

3.4 access.spec.ts:282 — the 900ms Band-D glide before the drawer census
    Cured with a new file-local `settledDrawer(page)`: the rail's rect read across
    two consecutive animation frames in ONE round trip, polled until they agree,
    reding with the reason if the glide never stops. The census that follows reads
    every control's painted box; a glide still running moves those boxes, and their
    positions ARE the figure.

3.5/3.6 board-covisibility.spec.ts:635 / :644 — the two fold controls
    Both are style-injection ablations (`max-width: 100dvh`, then `max-width: none`)
    followed by a one-shot overflow read. Cured with the settled-read idiom: the
    verdict is an `expect.poll(…).toMatchObject({…})` over the same `read()`, the
    returned object carrying `overflow`/`chromeAbove` so a red prints them. Control
    2's ablation — the arm that proves the bound is not a decoration — is no longer
    itself timing-dependent.

3.7 filter-census.spec.ts:111 — the 600ms "one settled beat past the bake"
    ASSERTED INVARIANT: the FILTERED POPULATION is still. The pose swap rides the
    estate's own scheduler beat rather than the WAAPI, so the `getAnimations()` poll
    directly above cannot see it and the sleep was standing in for it. Cured by
    polling `filteredCount(page)` until two consecutive reads agree — the census's
    own figure, held still. A grid still swapping poses in and out of the filtered
    set keeps disagreeing until it stops.

3.8 filter-census.spec.ts:376 — `assertNoHoverFilters()`'s stability precondition
    The old form sampled, slept 400ms, sampled again and asserted equality: it reds
    on a regime that is merely SLOW to settle and greens on one that drifts on a
    400ms period. Same two-agreeing-reads poll; stillness is now waited for and
    asserted as itself, and the regime name rides the failure message.

3.9 mobile-platform.spec.ts:494 — the `bottom: 0` keyboard-anchor ablation
    Re-anchoring the sheet re-lays it and re-runs the interior scroll `seat()`
    drives. Cured to a polled `toMatchObject({portStranded, chipStranded})` carrying
    `portBottom`/`bottom`/`bandEdge` in the object, so the ablation arm reds on the
    geometry rather than on the clock.

3.10/3.11 visual-regression.spec.ts:605 / :627 — the deal-receipt hover pair
    The old comment ("longer than any reveal transition the estate has owned") is a
    claim about the estate, not about this frame. Cured with a file-local
    `settledClearance(where)`: the verb/receipt boxes are re-read until `gap`,
    `axis` and `receiptW` all agree across two consecutive reads, and only the
    settled figure reaches the (unchanged) assertions — so a transient reveal is
    still caught, which a poll-to-true would have masked.

3.12/3.13 zone-grammar.spec.ts:468 / :475 — `collapse()`, both 80ms waits
    Both halves cured. The injected-rule wait becomes
    `expect.poll(min width|height).toBeLessThan(44)`; the post-revert wait becomes
    `expect.poll(…).toBeGreaterThanOrEqual(44)` — the shipped box coming back IS the
    settle, so it is the thing waited on, and the two dimension arms can no longer
    measure each other.
