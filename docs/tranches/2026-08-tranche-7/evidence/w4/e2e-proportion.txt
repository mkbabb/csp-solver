THE HARDEN, AND WHAT IT COST. Under `--workers=2` the mobile rows first went red on
`locator.click: Test ended` with Playwright's own log reading "element is not stable" ×30s: the
well's verb was clicked INTO the drawer's Band-D glide, and the retry loop ate the row's whole
deadline without the product ever misbehaving. Two cures, both in the spec: `openWell` settles
the card (the same polled two-agreeing-reads instrument the geometry rows use) before anything
is clicked, and `invite()` waits for the verb to be ENABLED (it is `:disabled` while a board is
in flight). The settle's poll cadence is pinned to [100,100,200] rather than the default ramp —
the default billed 1.5s of ramp for a 520ms glide and pushed the three rows to 28.2s / +5.4%,
outside the price. Re-verified after: 18/18 green at `--repeat-each=3 --workers=4`, and the
whole file 34/34 twice at `--workers=2`.

AGAINST THE PRICE. The wave priced +3 rows / +6 runs at ≈+44.5s serial and +4.1% on the whole
config. Measured: +19.6s and +3.7%. The wall-clock figures are a darwin box's and a CI runner's
are slower, but the RATIO is the claim, and it lands inside the priced envelope. The file's own
share of the config's serial cost goes 9.6% → 12.9%, and it is the estate's most expensive spec
either way. The two mobile rows are now the most
expensive rows in it (a phone boots the whole estate into a quarter of the width), and the
reload row is the cheapest thing added.
