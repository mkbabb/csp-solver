Instrument: `git diff --numstat <base> <head>` — a TREE-TO-TREE diff, not a sum of per-commit
diffs. Tree-to-tree is what "the tranche changed the estate by N lines" means; summing
commits double-counts every line a later commit rewrote (the two differ by ~36/~36 here).
No `-w`, no rename detection tuning, defaults throughout, so anyone re-runs it and gets this.

WHAT THIS IS NOT
----------------

seventeen owner marks and a debug lane is parsimonious is the owner's question — this file
only makes sure the question gets asked against real numbers. The fewer-lines law binds each
lane's cut; three of six lanes cut, three grew, and the record said all six cut.
