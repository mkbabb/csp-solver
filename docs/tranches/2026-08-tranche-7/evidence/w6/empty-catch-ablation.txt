legacy sees "warm().catch(async () => {});" -> BLIND

Why it cannot match at any offset: on `.catch(() => {`, the optional param group `\([^)]*\)`
opens at the handler's own `(` and `[^)]*` halts at the arrow parens' `)`, so the following
`\{` lands on `=`; dropping the group puts `\{` on `(`. No threshold, allowlist or exception
would ever have reached this form — only a second alternative does. Hence: the promise-handler
grammar is now a first-class arm of the head, and the frozen old regex rides in the self-test
as the discriminating control, so the cure has to keep proving itself at every run.
