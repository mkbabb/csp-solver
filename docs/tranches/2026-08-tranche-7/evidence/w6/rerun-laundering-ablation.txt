                           attempt banks as `unknown` (refused downstream, never
                           permissive). Prints a warning when its own green is not
                           the sha's only conclusion.

                           1. A missing field refuses (an artifact from the older
                           generator is exactly the blindness being cured). The one
                           lift is `reruns_permitted yes` + a `reruns_reason` line,
                           hand-added — the generator never writes either.
