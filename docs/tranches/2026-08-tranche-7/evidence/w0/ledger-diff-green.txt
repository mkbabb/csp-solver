WHAT THE RE-BANK CHANGED
  The first bank of this file was green on a grammar narrower than the estate's own ids. ANY_ID_CELL
  wanted `-\d+` at a cell's end, so `T7-R15`, `T7-R13a`, `P-5e` and `EL-3a` never parsed as rows: the
  report printed "§6 … 32 rows" over 62 on disk and left 30 rows outside every arm — a false number
  inside the instrument's own output, which is the class the instrument exists to catch. Three fixes,
  none of them a weakened arm:
    · The id grammar reads the population, not a guess at it: a family, an ordinal that may carry the
      recap `R` (`T7-R13`) and a lettered sub-row (`P-5e`, `EL-3a`), and slash-lists (`CH-47/48/49`).
      An input cell may also carry the row's own title, which is how DISPOSITIONS §3-§7 writes it.
      §6 now counts 62 — 19 + 11 + 13 + 19, its four subsections.
    · ROW_FAMILIES widened from CH/PR/U/S to every family the estate tables: + O, P, N, B, D, Q, EL,
      BAL and the tranche-scoped `T<n>-R`. The ORPHAN arm's input went 48 row ids → 142, the whole
      DISPOSITIONS corpus (120 rows, CH-47/48/49 expanding to three, plus the 20-ballot sheet).
    · --owner-block stopped truncating and stopped printing "(unstated)" over a firing default. A
      balloted row carries a default, not a trigger; the block prints it, labeled `default:`. A close
      record embeds this output verbatim, so a cell cut at a column width is a state amputated
      mid-phrase — "BALLOT → BAL-01, dispatched 2026-08-03," was the whole of CH-35's state.
