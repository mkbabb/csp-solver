> land from this prompt. The deliverable is the next tranche, fully formed: plan

## Standing edicts (binding on the formed tranche)

- NO quick solutions or workarounds — idiomatic, gestalt, architectural
  transposition.
- NO legacy code — clean breaks; no aliases, shims, dual paths, or masking
  fallbacks.
- Every chronically deferred item folded as a DECIDED row (build / fold / retire,
  with rationale). Re-booking forbidden. A chronic riding ≥2 closes un-decided is a
  disease row whose deciding is a wave of its own.
- Recap ALL prompts: an unaddressed ask becomes a registry row with an owning wave.
  Silent drops forbidden.

- 32 agents as a steerable budget; a diverse audit-lens portfolio (plan-vs-landed,
  gate soundness — can each gate fail, gestalt vs per-mechanism PASS,
  chronic/disposition ledgers, prompt recap, consumer truth, performance,
  accessibility, doc drift, dead-code/dual-path, cross-repo).
- Withhold the success narrative from most auditors.
- An explicit registry of finding families by MECHANISM; adversarial throughout —
  the close-class lies hunted by name (green-over-broken, vacuous-green gates,

  per-mechanism green over gestalt broken).
- Concrete deliverables only: file:line, failing probe, repro, named row.
- The registry is stable when two consecutive passes surface nothing new.
- Model routing: Fable owns orchestration/synthesis/adjudication and ALL design
  through DesignSync; Opus takes fanout with the model declared at every spawn;
