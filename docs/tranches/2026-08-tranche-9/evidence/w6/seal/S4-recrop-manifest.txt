THE METHOD, AND WHY IT IS THE ONE THE POLICY NAMES
  The gate's footer: "Recrop to the pixels under audit; text-first for anything a
  number can state." Two transforms, both applied per file and recorded per file below.

  1. RECROP — the 22 gallery-pi deck frames only. A pi frame proves a rect census, and
     the rect is the deck. 1248x685 whole-viewport captures carried the new-game panel
     and the page's bottom furniture, which no claim in gallery-3D.md §4 touches. The
     crop is 1104x520+72+0, IDENTICAL on every frame so every before/after/after-rerun
     pair still compares. It holds both declared ring states whole: the scrollport ring
     (image px ~97,23 -> 1152,479) and the active-option ring at idx4 (~801,31 ->
     1152,464) and at idx0, plus the pips row at y~502. Verified by eye on both arms.

  2. RE-ENCODE — pngquant --quality=80-98 --speed 1, 256 colours. GEOMETRY UNCHANGED:
     no downscale anywhere, because these banks' claims are pixel offsets (R15's
     352px -> 191px, R13's element-scoped ink, the ring perimeter) and a resample would
     put the ruler out of true. Measured fidelity on the worst case: RMSE 0.41% of full
     scale, which is the anti-aliasing moving a level or two on line art.

     ImageMagick stamps date:create/date:modify tEXt chunks on write, which made frames
     that had been BYTE-IDENTICAL diverge. Those chunks were stripped at the chunk level
     (IDAT untouched) and all six identity relations came back exactly as banked:
       after/pi-nofocus-idx4-{chromium,webkit}    == before/pi-nofocus-idx4-{chromium,webkit}
       after-rerun/ring-idx4-{chromium,webkit}    == after/ring-idx4-{chromium,webkit}
       after-rerun/wordmark-{chromium,webkit}     == after/wordmark-{chromium,webkit}
     So gallery-pi-ledger.txt's IDENTICAL rows are still re-verifiable ON THE BANK.

  UNTOUCHED, on purpose (10 files, byte-for-byte as captured):
    - the 6 gallery-pi wordmark frames (1022x120, element-scoped, 16-20 KB, under cap).
      gallery-pi-ledger.txt cites sha a7c141068382 / d1badb26b346 for two of them and
      those digests stay LIVE against the files on disk.
    - the 4 r13-frames/card-* crops. They are what 6d-r13.md:74 cites, the claim is a
      COLOUR (a peer's 7 goes green while the reader's 4 stays blue), and they are
      already the element-scoped crop, all under cap.
