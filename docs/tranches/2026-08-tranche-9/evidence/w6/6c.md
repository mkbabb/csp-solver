So it is **INTERMITTENT under whole-suite load, not terminal**, and **not CPU contention** —
saturating every core does not move it. "Terminal" is what makes a row safe to stop thinking
about, and it was wrong.

### The real defect is what the red never said

The failure reads `Expected "gallery-card-1", Received "gallery-card-0"`. **One bit**, out of a
gesture standing on four independent facts, and two entirely different mechanisms produce that
same bit:

- **A** — the gesture was never taken as a drag (`onPointerUp` early-returns; nothing computed).
- **A′** — the drag was held and the step computed ZERO, so `onSnap` re-published the card the
  deck was already on and mutated nothing.
- **B** — the step was taken and WALKED BACK. This one is a live product hazard the composable
  names in its own prose: at the three-slot rung cards 0 and 1 **share** a rest position
  (`targetScrollLeft` clamps both to 0), so anything re-deriving the index from position alone
  is deciding on evidence that cannot discriminate.

A, A′ and B want different cures and the old row could not tell them apart. That is how it spent
a campaign as "the wanderer": **an unfalsifiable red is a red nobody can act on.**

### The disposition: a TEST CURE that makes the next red a diagnosis

No product change (the pi law holds — this moves no pixel), and no shrug either.

1. **The row asserts its own PREMISE.** `-260` lands on card 1 only because the step is
   `Math.round(travel / slot)` over a 352px slot at the three-slot rung. Rung, card count, slot
   and anchor are now checked before the gesture, so a LAYOUT change reds as a layout change.
2. **The row carries a LEDGER.** `armSnapLedger` records the `aria-activedescendant` trail and
   whether the release was held as a drag — installed via `addInitScript`, so arming costs the
   gesture nothing (a probe that slows the beat it measures is measuring a different beat). The
   trail is the assertion's failure message, and it names A, A′ or B and which file to open.

**Plant-proof, both discriminations** (`holdouts/wanderer-ledger-plant.txt`):

- press lands off the deck → `drag trail: [1938ms release as a click (never dragged)]` →
  **MECHANISM A**;
- a real 20px drag whose step rounds to 0 → `drag trail: [579ms release AS A DRAG]`, aria trail
  empty → **MECHANISM A′**.

Two instrument defects were caught by those plants rather than shipped:

- the first ledger read `is-dragging` from a `MutationObserver` callback, which runs AFTER the
  whole batch — the add and the remove both read the settled (absent) state and the toggle was
  invisible. Sampling from a **capture-phase** `pointerup` on `window`, which runs before the
  composable's own bubble listener, reads the flag while it still stands.
- the init script observed `document.documentElement` before there was one; the throw took the
  REST OF THE SCRIPT with it and reported an empty drag trail for a gesture that had plainly
  dragged — one step from sending the diagnosis to the wrong mechanism. Order is load-bearing
  and now says so in the file.

A third fact fell out of the first plant and is worth the record: at zero edge air the card under
the **viewport's middle is card 1**, not the aria-centered card 0 — so a press-without-drag there
is a flank warp to card 1, not a null gesture. That is why a 3px "plant" passed.
