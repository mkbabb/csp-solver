## The two that taught something

**K1 — the side door was the finding, not the dead slot.** The fence named a loader with zero
consumers. The reason it had none is that `App.vue` read `card.eager`, and then mounted a spec
it had imported ITSELF: the default game never went through the row the table was iterating, so
the registry had a loading story its own principal case did not use. Three artifacts, one
disagreement — a loader nobody called, a boolean four of five rows did not carry, and a mount
path around the table. `mount: AnyGameSpec | (() => Promise<AnyGameSpec>)` ends all three: the
SHAPE of the value is the chunking claim, so there is no second claim left to drift. The static
`sudokuSpec` import moved out of `App.vue` and into `cards.ts`, which is where `tdz-probe.mjs`
already counts the one main-chunk ride.

**K5 — the guard could not fire, twice over.** `linux-webkit-bake-quarantine.ts` parked 15 rows
and cited "THIRD PINNING (CH-62)" in three files. CH-62 has been RETIRED since T8 formation, and
the park early-returns off `process.platform !== "linux"` while O-12 took every browser lane out
of CI at `d1daefb3`. Either fact alone empties it. What it still did was subtract 15 rows from
two count floors — `theme-bake-webkit`'s floor was **0**, a project that was a name and not a
gate. The rows returned and they ASSERT: 26/26 green, both engines
(`kills-final/returned-rows-run.txt`). Their floors move through W5's restamp (`6a-5`), never by
hand.
