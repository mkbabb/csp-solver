  31ms  the frame is already 640 and the engine has already adjusted scrollLeft to 212 — and
        `aria-activedescendant` is ALREADY card-1. Nothing the composable writes has run yet.
        The only seam that can publish a choice that fast is `scrollend` -> `reportSnap`
        (UNDEBOUNCED; the 90ms debounce is on `scroll` alone). `restingIndex()` reads 212
        against the NEW 640 layout, answers 1, and `onSnap(1)` -> `syncFromScroll(1)` takes the
        choice and dismisses the ribbon.

  90ms  NOW the ResizeObserver fires `jumpTo(currentIndex)` — and `currentIndex` is 1, because
        `reportSnap` assigned it 59ms ago. snap drops to `none` and the deck is pinned to
        `targetScrollLeft(1)` = 352. The re-pin is not the defect: it faithfully re-pins a
        choice that was already stolen.

  103ms the rAF re-arm restores `x mandatory` on a position that is already a snap point.
        Nothing re-snaps after it.

`currentIndex`, which is 1 by the time the observer runs, so it re-pins the WRONG card twice
