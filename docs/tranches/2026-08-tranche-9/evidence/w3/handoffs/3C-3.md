# 3C-3 — §3.7 presence-timeout roster truth needs a clock the panel cannot see

Lane 3C's fence lists "presence-timeout roster truth (the row stops lying past the 45s expiry)"
under `GameControlPanel.vue`. The panel renders `session.players.value` and holds no clock, no
per-peer last-seen, and no way to derive either. The cure is `useSession.ts` + `relayWire.ts`,
both outside 3C's fence.

## The defect, in the estate's own words

`useSession.ts:825-830`, the header on `CUR_EXPIRY_MS`:

> A silent peer's ghost expires. The socket that stays OPEN behind a dead page is nobody's
> `bye` (the presence timeout is cut-2's, `relayWire.ts`'s own header), so without this a cursor
> could sit on a cell for as long as the tab was dead — a ghost with nobody behind it.
> **The roster row still lies for that page; this at least stops the board from doing it.**

`relayWire.ts:35-40` carries the other half of the same admission: connection-derived presence
left with trystero, `relay.ts`'s `announceLeave` covers the socket that CLOSES, and "a socket
that stays OPEN behind a dead page is nobody's `bye` yet, and wants the presence timeout flagged
here: cut-2's, not this."

So the row is written down twice, in two files, as a known lie with a named owner. §3.7 is that
owner arriving.

## Why the obvious cure is wrong, and it must be said before anyone writes it

`armCursorExpiry` (`useSession.ts:858-861`) already holds a 45s per-peer timer. It is tempting
to prune the roster on the same clock. **Do not.** That timer is armed by `cur` frames alone
(`kind === "cur"`), and `cur` is sent only when a peer MOVES their focus. A peer who is present,
reading, and not moving sends nothing for 45s and would be evicted from the roster while they
are sitting right there — trading a row that lies about a dead page for a row that lies about a
live one. A cursor clock is not a presence clock.

## The seam

Presence needs a frame that says "still here" whether or not anything happened. The estate has
exactly one idempotent presence frame already: `hi` (`relayWire.ts:89`, `:213`, and the header's
"either idempotent presence (`hi`, re-sent on every reconnect) or a stamped op"). Re-sending it
on an interval is the whole mechanism, and `useSession.ts` already has the receiving side —
`h.peer(from, true)` at `relayWire.ts:263` — so an arriving `hi` from a known peer is already
"they are still here".

**`web/frontend/src/games/shared/relayWire.ts`, beside `RETRY_MS` (`:57`)** — add

```
/** Presence, said on a clock rather than inferred from traffic. A socket that stays OPEN behind
 *  a dead page is nobody's `bye` (this header's own admission, above), so the only honest signal
 *  is one every LIVE page keeps sending. `hi` is already idempotent presence and already the
 *  re-request the room answers with the whole board, so the beat is the frame this file already
 *  has rather than a fifth kind. 15s gives the 45s roster expiry three misses before it acts. */
const HEARTBEAT_MS = 15000;
```

and in the subscribe block that already sends the first `hi` (`relayWire.ts:213`), arm the beat
and clear it in the teardown beside `removeEventListener("pagehide", bye)` (`:273`).

**`web/frontend/src/games/shared/useSession.ts`** — stamp `known`/`present` on every `hi` and
drop a peer from `present` (never from `known` — their digits keep their colour, `:321-323`)
once `Date.now() - lastSeen > PRESENCE_EXPIRY_MS`, with `PRESENCE_EXPIRY_MS = 45000` so the
roster and the cursor expire on ONE number rather than two that can drift.
