**The number it must move.** The cold-boot woff2 census: Playwright WebKit issues **7 GETs, all
HTTP 200 with full bodies**—firacode ×2, patrickhand ×2, fraunces ×3 (preload 22 ms, `bakeFace`
fetch 94 ms, `@font-face` 130 ms): 22,872 B of preload miss plus a 14,936 B bake re-fetch.
Chromium issues 4 (the extra fraunces is a 300 B memory-cache hit on a true first visit, a full
re-download under cache-disabled). Causal, by ablation: stripping the three preload hints moves
**WebKit board-ready 237.0 → 158.0 ms (−79.0, −18.9 KB)**; a font-abort arm agrees at −77.0. The
same strip **costs chromium +23.5 ms** (333.5 → 357.0 at 4×).
Instruments: `attribution/A2/webkit-font-confirm.mjs` (the DRIVER's request stream with
per-response status, timing-free, load-proof); `attribution/refute/A2/xablate.mjs --mode
nopreload|fontabort`.

**FIRST ACT, before a line of `src/`**: 8.3's probe reads the woff2 resource count on the real
device. Playwright WebKit served by `vite preview` is not Safari on the CF edge; if Safari
consumes the preload, the first half of this charter is void and only the bake half stands.

**The mechanism.** Scheduling and request de-duplication, two halves:
1. make the `@font-face` fetch and the `<link rel="preload" as="font" crossorigin>` resolve to
   ONE request in WebKit (same URL, same credentials mode, declaration order), or—if the engine
   will not match—emit the hints only where they're honoured. Never the engine-blind strip.
2. `bakeFace()` (`HandwrittenLogo.vue:35`) bakes from bytes the page already holds instead of a
   fresh `fetch`, and its chunked `String.fromCharCode` + `btoa` leaves the first-paint window.
   The bake stays; `loadBakeFace`'s fallback contract stays.

**The π obligation.** The same three subsets ship, letter-exact (the T8 ransom-note lesson);
the wordmark bakes with the real Fraunces face exactly as today—per-pose hash equal. Goldens
unmoved; `filterBudget` 9.

**Prove it on.** WebKit cold desk + mobile (count, and board-ready interleaved base/cured, n ≥ 5);
chromium 4× cold and true-first-visit (must not lose its 23.5 ms); then the device.

**Accept.** max requests per subset ≤ 1 in both engines (an engine that can't report prints NOT
MEASURED, never PASS); WebKit board-ready down by about the ablation's 79 ms; chromium not
worse than spread; wordmark hashes equal.

**It must not.** Drop or narrow a subset; delete the data-URI bake; let the wordmark paint a
fallback face longer than today (the WebKit FOUT window, ~130 ms, is W7's to judge).
