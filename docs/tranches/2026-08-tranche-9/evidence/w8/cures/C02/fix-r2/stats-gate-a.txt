WHAT IT IS. The warm floored at 3,000 ms runs 3.0 → 3.7 s of page time. Unthrottled the rig's
window is [board-ready + 1,200, board-ready + 4,200] ≈ [1.3 s, 4.5 s]: the warm is inside it.
On chromium at this pose (1440×900 dpr 1) no warm frame reaches 33.4 ms and the census reads 0.
On WebKit the same poses cost 35–48 ms each — one or two frames per window, against a gate whose
allowance is zero.

WHY IT CANNOT BE PLACED ELSEWHERE, in the two censuses' own terms:
  · TBT(3000) forbids a task over 50 ms in [0, 3,000] from navigation (repair round 1's finding).
  · GATE A forbids a frame over 33.4 ms in [board-ready + 1,200, board-ready + 4,200].
  · Unthrottled board-ready is 0.1–0.3 s, so the two spans overlap into ONE forbidden run from 0
    to ~4.5 s. Throttled they still touch: at 4× CPU board-ready is ~1.45 s, so the idle span is
    [2.65 s, 5.65 s] and the TBT span closes at 3.0 s inside it.
  · The first tap of the instrument that reads this cure lands EARLIER than the far edge: at 4×
    desk the probe's first toggle bakes at 5.35 s against board-ready 1.55 s (fix-r1 raws,
    base-w1), i.e. board-ready + 3.8 s, inside the idle span.
  So there is no placement before the flip that is outside both, and a pose cannot be split under
  33.4 ms: the library's own reading (vue.ts, "WHY A CACHE AND NOT A THREAD") is that the encode
  does not leave the main thread, and every way of making a pose cheaper is on the charter's
  REFUSED list (DPR, captureSide, poseCount, grain-static).

THE TRADE, stated for the chair and not decided here: one or two 35–48 ms frames, once, on a page
nobody is touching, against ~900 ms off the first theme flip. GATE A is a LOCAL instrument (O-12:
CI is sixteen browserless lanes; perf-subset was deleted at d1daefb3), and its ceiling was written
for PERMANENT idle churn — T4-P1's unlayered-SVG filter re-execution, which never stopped. A
bounded one-time warm is a different animal that the gate cannot distinguish. Either the trade is
taken in writing, or this whole family — C02, C11's persistence, any idle memoization — is closed
by GATE A.
