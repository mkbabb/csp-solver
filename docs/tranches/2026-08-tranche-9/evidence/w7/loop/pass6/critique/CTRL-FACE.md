### 2.1 The parked publisher never unparks when no transition starts (product defect, both engines)

`toggleKeys` sets `keysFolding = true` whenever the fold's duration is above 0. Only `onFoldSettled` clears it, on a `grid-template-rows` `transitionend`/`transitioncancel`. With two presses in one task, the net state doesn't change and no transition starts, so no event ever arrives. From then on the bar observer is parked, and the content observer only calls `publishFold`.

`park-stuck.mjs` (1280×800, normal motion). After the presses, the root font was set to 18 px:

| engine | single press (control path) | two presses in one task |
