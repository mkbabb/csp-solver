## 9 · Risks, stated as the synthesizer must weigh them

1. **The marks wrap is a second grammar.** One group's field wraps and six do not. The
   ONE-system claim survives only if the wrap is declared as a field property (`the field
   wraps when it must`) rather than a marks exception. The gallery's `nowrap` discipline
   is the counter-argument: it made wrapping illegal *on purpose* so a shortfall would
   measure. Choose one and gate it.
2. **`checking` is the binding word and copy is not this lane's.** Every 390 number turns
   on 121.47px of one string. If M16/the owner would take a shorter name the budget opens
   by up to 21px, but renaming to green a layout is the circularity the chair struck
   elsewhere. Price it; do not do it silently.
3. **Arm (b) still owes the height ledger.** Pass-1's `781 / 628` for arm (b) at the dock
   was read off a prototype whose margin had *collapsed* at 390 — it is not a margin-held
   figure and must not be cited as one. The prototype re-derives it against HEAD's 699/628
   plus the +27 wrap, not against arm (a)'s 999 projection.
4. **ROW 3 and the gallery pull opposite ways.** The estate's shipped margin column runs
   at ratio 1.00; arm (b) in the card must run at ≥1.23 with a 121.47px track. The two
