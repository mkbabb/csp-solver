### 2.4 The overrun — three bases, and an engine split nobody has reported

At HEAD the mark is `` `${len+1}ch` `` on the button. Measured with the fonts loaded
(`document.fonts.ready`), both engines:

| Patrick Hand 20px | 8.9594 | 8.9594 | — |

**WebKit resolves `ch` to exactly 0.5em on the two faces whose cuts carry no `0`, and agrees
with Chromium only on Patrick Hand (whose subset holds `U+0030-0039`, `index.css:95`).** The
shipped scribble under a 3-character chip is therefore **48.0078px in Chromium and 40.00px in

The overrun ratios that follow:

| basis | HEAD (Fira Code) chromium | HEAD webkit | re-faced to the hand (both engines, identical) |
|---|---|---|---|
| mark ÷ element content box | 1.1665 – 1.4990 | 0.9722 – 1.2498 | — |
| mark ÷ `TextMetrics.width` (advance) | **1.1668 – 1.5000** | **0.9722 – 1.2498** | **1.2164 – 1.9310** |
| mark ÷ glyph ink | 1.1937 – 1.5989 | *= advance* | *= advance* |

Three facts for the gate:

- On a MONOSPACE face `(len+1)ch ÷ (len × ch)` is exactly `(n+1)/n` — 1.5 / 1.333 / 1.25 / 1.2 /

  same vacuity wearing a different constant (critique F4).
- **WebKit's `TextMetrics.actualBoundingBox*` returns the ADVANCE box** — ink == advance for all
  11 chips, both faces, both directions. *There is no cross-engine glyph-ink basis.* A band cut
  on glyph ink is a chromium-only band.
- **Re-facing the chips to the hand CURES the engine split** (Patrick Hand has digits, so `ch`

| input | safari 16.4 | safari 16.3 | safari 15 | safari 13 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `margin-top:-1lh` | passed through | passed through | passed through | **passed through** |

the declared floor must meet it — re-derived, the way check 2 re-derives Lightning's.

### 2.10 `@property` and the no-fallback law (chair §6.5) — the constraint it collides with

`CSS.registerProperty`, both engines:

| initial-value for `syntax: "<length>"` | chromium | webkit |
|---|---|---|
| `14.048px` | ok | ok |
| `clamp(12px, 3vw, 16px)` | ok | ok |
| `0.878rem` | **SyntaxError: not computationally independent** | same |
| `1em` | **SyntaxError** | same |
| `var(--type-tag)` | **SyntaxError: does not parse** | same |
| omitted | **SyntaxError: mandatory** | same |
| `syntax: "*"`, omitted | ok | ok |

Three consequences:

1. CTRL-TAPE's `--washi-tag-rung` graft cannot take `--type-tag` as its registered initial —
   `--type-tag` is `var(--type-caption)` = `clamp(0.75rem, 0.71rem + 0.21vw, 1rem)`
   (`typography.css:31, 123`), a **rem-bearing** clamp, rejected. A registered rung forks to an
   absolute-px literal (14.048px at the desk, 14.00 at the dock — they differ) or to a
   vw-only clamp, or it takes `syntax: "*"` and forgoes §6.5's fail-at-computed-value-time
   guarantee entirely. **Name which, in the spec.**
2. The `--face-*` tokens are not a registrable `<length>`/`<color>`; `syntax: "*"` is the only
   practical type, so they carry no initial value and §6.5's guarantee does not reach them.
   Measured analogue: an unregistered token consumed with no fallback makes the declaration
   invalid at computed-value time and the property takes its OWN initial — `width` → `auto`.
   For `font-family` that is the **UA default**, i.e. a missing `--face-printed` paints the eight
   names in Times, and a static gate cannot see it. That is a born-RED row worth writing: delete
   the token, assert the names still compute Fraunces.
3. `--ring-ink` does not exist at HEAD. `var(--ring-ink)` with no fallback makes `outline-color`
   invalid at computed-value time → the outline paints `currentColor`, silently, which happens
   to be what pass 2's fallback said. **Consuming a token §6 has not minted is a no-op dressed
   as a law**; the row is "measure it when §6 lands", and until then the family reports the
   chip's own ring ratio and asserts nothing.
