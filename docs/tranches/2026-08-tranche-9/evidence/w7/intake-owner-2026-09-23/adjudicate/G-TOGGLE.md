## 2 · The apotheosis

**The page turns once, on a hinge.** The press squashes the button (120, kept). The sun folds flat on
`lift` for one `note`, twisting to −15° with its sparkles riding inside the fold, opaque to the last
frame, and is cut at the park. One `whisper` after the press the theme flips: the five grounds dusk on
their own verb from there, and every surface that snaps today snaps at the page's own start as it does
today. The board's ink does not snap and does not join: it turns graphite-to-chalk in ONE frame, `0.343 ×
dusk` after the flip going dark and `0.249 × dusk` going light — the instant old and new ink read equally
on the darkening paper. The moon is born opaque at the park the frame the sun is cut, stands up on
springPop for one `throw`, crests 48 ms after the page lands, and its stars pop on the crest one beat
apart, each on a `whisper`. The live body boils the whole way through on the rest stack's own four
fields at the sky's bands, so both hand-offs are field-identical. Nothing is ever translucent: pencil is
on the page or it isn't. PRM is one cut, the toggle included.

```
ms   0      120     150        237/270     250  253        500   548   673   798   948
     |press  |squash |FLIP      |ink HINGE  |cut |page half |page |crest|star |star |star ● settle
     |=== fold (lift, note 250) — opaque, accents ride ===|
                     |=========== dusk (350, ease; five grounds) ==========|
                     |    ink: old ─────────────┤ new (one frame; D→L +237, L→D +270)
                                                |●born 0.06 |==== stand-up (springPop, throw 520) ====|
                                                                          ✦     ✦     ✦   (whisper each)
```

Three events land inside one sixth of a beat: the fold's flat frame and the moon's birth (+250), the
page's midpoint (+253) and the ink's hinge (+237 / +270). That coincidence is the design — the ink's turn
IS the page's turn, and no frame holds two bodies, old ink on new paper, or a smudge.

## 7 · Gaps and risks (a gap is a gap)

- Nothing was served or painted in the portfolio or here; every number in §1 is arithmetic from tokens
  and curves, and every gate has n = 0 until the prototype runs it.
- **R-2 (hinge timing):** the hinge assumes the ink's delayed transition and the grounds' dusk start in
  the same style-change event. Both carry `!important` under `html.theme-turning`, as the five grounds
  do today, so they should; if either engine starts them a frame apart the worst frame drops to ~2.9–3.0.
  G4 reads it; a one-frame miss is a RED, not a rounding.
- **R-3 (painted ≠ token):** the grid strokes paint at partial opacity; the painted hinge minimum sits
  under the token's 3.11 and may be arithmetically impossible ≥ 3.0 for thin sub-grid strokes on ANY
  schedule. G4 prints the painted minimum against its bound; the chair rules the number.
