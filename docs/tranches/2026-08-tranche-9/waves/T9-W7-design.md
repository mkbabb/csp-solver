## The census, restated as design questions

1. **One heading voice** — five identical option groups, three typographic voices
   (25.9px Fraunces H2 · 14.4px Patrick Hand washi tag · 14.4px zone-row-label at 68%
   alpha; only two are document headings). Design the hierarchy law: what a group
   heading IS in this product, applied five-for-five. (The document-structure half —
   headings that are headings — is W3's; the look is decided here, together.)
2. **A button system** — four treatments in one card, three more on the mobile
   toolbar. One graded system: primary act / option row / icon tool / info whisper,
   each with its drawn idiom, applied everywhere.
3. **The accent family** — at rest the product is one warm hue (99.2% of chromatic
   content); on touch, four unrelated stock accents appear (two blues 15° apart doing
   different jobs; violet nowhere else). Draw the interactive accents from the house
   palette, or declare the contrast-bearing exceptions and make them kin.
4. **The fill meter explained** — an unlabeled violet stripe straddling the board
   frame, growing per keystroke. Either it explains itself (placement, form, a first-run
   whisper) or it carries a visible label; its hue joins §3's family.
5. **The wobble law extended** — the selection ring and peer wash are the only
   CAD-precise marks on a hand-drawn board (ring σ 0.00px vs grid σ 2.5px). The
   house's pencil idiom (pre-baked path variants) reaches the most-watched marker.
   π-guard: the boil budget census (filterBudget 9) must not grow.
6. **Focus rings in the house hand** — geometric rects today. Redesigned within the
   non-negotiable a11y floor: visible, high-contrast, consistent. (The deck ring's
   MOTION — riding the viewport, never the focused card — is W3's correctness row;
   its look is decided here.)
7. **The hint note's lifecycle** — "only N fits here" persists at full opacity through
   every later act. Design the retraction: what dismisses a margin note, how it ages.
   (The conflict-note TRUTH — naming the violated unit — is W1's; this is the note's
   life, not its words.)
8. **Mobile tabs made tabs** — size/level as two headings with no affordance, options
   0×0 until discovered, the shared option row spanning under both. Make the control
   legible as what it is.
9. **B1 executes** (fired or resolved): "the solver finishes the board" and
   `solver's answer N` recut to the M16 register.
10. **The controls system re-cut** (T9-M03/M04/M05, the fired B6 scope): section
    titles larger and PROPERLY STICKY (mechanics in W2 §2.6, the voice here); the
    mobile floating bar gains its proper border in the house hand; the whole card's
    hierarchy designed as one system — group, title, option, act — desktop and
    mobile the same grammar. §1 and §2 execute INSIDE this system, not beside it.
11. **The masthead telemetry** (T9-M08): your name + multiplayer presence "displayed
    deftly next to the mbabb logo," both platforms. The owner sketched two shapes —
